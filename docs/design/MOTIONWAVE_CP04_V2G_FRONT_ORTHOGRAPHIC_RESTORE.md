## Source geometry

- Six-part V2D toggle generated by design-assets/motionwave-ui-lock/v2d/cp04_v2d_repo_generator.py. Includes captured pivot ball, upper and lower eyelet, mounting washer/nut, shaft and separate insulating tip. OFF/ON at ±21 degrees.
- Three-part V2E molded knob generated by design-assets/motionwave-ui-lock/v2e/cp04_v2e_knob_cad.py. Forty real grip channels and recessed ivory inlay in three states.
- New Blender/Cycles-ready lookdev scene source: design-assets/motionwave-ui-lock/v2g/blender_cp04_front.py.
- Local conversation ZIP: MotionWave_CP04_V2G_Real3D_FrontFace_Complete.zip. Contains 10 STEP/GLB files (five states), actual front-view RGBA previews and the separately tested VTK renderer. These binary files are not claimed as committed to GitHub.

## Render contract

Blender camera is directly above the Z axis at (0,0,100), has rotation (0,0,0), and uses ORTHOGRAPHIC projection. The manufacturing panel lies in XY and local +Y remains screen-up. The toggle emerges toward the viewer; never substitute a side, oblique, or three-quarter product shot.

Generate the STEP and GLB models:

    python design-assets/motionwave-ui-lock/v2d/cp04_v2d_repo_generator.py review-cad/toggle
    python design-assets/motionwave-ui-lock/v2e/cp04_v2e_knob_cad.py review-cad/knob

After installing Blender 4.x, render editable scenes:

    blender --background --python design-assets/motionwave-ui-lock/v2g/blender_cp04_front.py -- --model review-cad/toggle/MW_CONTROL_006_V2D_OFF.glb --kind toggle --render review-out/toggle-off.png --blend review-out/toggle-off.blend

Repeat for ON and for knob MIN/MID/MAX with the appropriate GLB and kind. The Blender renderer source is NOT locally executed or visually approved; Blender was not installed in the development workspace.

## What has actually been tested

The separate locally rendered V2G preview uses the existing CAD files. Its independent verifier ran 110 passing geometry, alpha, SHA-256, output-scale and exact camera-orientation assertions. That was a VTK preview with a provisional multi-light approximation, not a calibrated physically based Blender/Cycles result. It is not commercial-quality signoff.

## Remaining gates

1. True front-facing physical assembly with readable lower eyelet, captured pivot and separate external ball tip.
2. Commercial phenolic/molded-polymer grain, brushed metal and ivory material response with broad restrained reflection rather than glossy vector styling.
3. Reproducible 2048px studio renders; real 390px phone and 1440px desktop captures; crisp 1x/2x/3x outputs and clean alpha.
4. All live control directions, host automation, DAW presets and DSP behaviors remain unchanged.
5. Owner must visually approve the three hero controls before any import into production.
