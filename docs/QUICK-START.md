# Quick Start

MotionLab Studio runs entirely in your browser. Nothing to install, no
account — open the app and press Play.

**https://txpps-motionlab-studio.roan-crest.workers.dev**

## Five minutes to a beat

1. **Hear the demo.** Press **Space** (or the Play button). The demo project
   plays synths, drums and audio loops through the mixer.
2. **Make a drum beat.** Click **+ Track → Drum Rack Track**. The pad grid
   opens in the Synth tab — click pads to hear them, then open the
   **Piano Roll** tab and draw hits on the pad keys (C1 upward).
3. **Add a bassline.** **+ Track → Instrument Track**, pick a preset in the
   Synth tab, and record from the on-screen keys (or draw notes in the
   piano roll). The A–L computer keys play the selected instrument.
4. **Drop in a loop.** Open **Browser → Loops** and tap one — it lands on a
   new audio track at the playhead. Drag its edges to trim; right-click for
   fades and edit commands.
5. **Mix and export.** Balance levels in the **Mixer** tab, then
   **⋯ → Export mix as WAV**.

## Recording audio

Arm an audio track (the **●** button on the track header), allow microphone
access when asked, then press the transport **Record**. A count-in plays,
the take lands as a clip, and additional passes stack as takes you can comp
(swipe across take lanes to choose the best parts).

## Sampling

Drag any sample from **Browser → Samples** onto a Quick Sampler's drop
area or a Drum Rack pad. Trim with the waveform handles (edges snap to
zero crossings), **Detect transients**, then **Slices → pads** to play the
pieces from a drum rack, or **Slices → MIDI** to replay them as a clip.

## Where your work lives

Projects autosave to this browser's storage (IndexedDB) a moment after
every change, and every save keeps a backup of the previous version.
Closing the tab with unsaved changes asks first. Use **Browser →
Projects** to save-as, duplicate, rename and switch projects, and
**⋯ → Export mix as WAV** to get audio out.

Press **?** anytime for the full keyboard shortcut list.
