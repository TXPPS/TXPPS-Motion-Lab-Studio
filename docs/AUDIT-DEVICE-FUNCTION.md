# Device function audit — does each device do what it shows?

**Describes commit `566e083e8f`.** History — a record of one tree at one
moment, which cannot go stale because it was never a claim about now. Superseded by [`audit/PLUGIN_AUDIT.md`](audit/PLUGIN_AUDIT.md), which covers what this did not.

Every stock device checked against its own audio: each declared parameter traced to the
line that reads it, each unit and range checked against what the DSP does with the number,
each face checked against the graph it claims to draw, each bypass checked for
transparency, and the offline render checked against the live one.

Scope: the 27 kinds in `EFFECT_SPECS` (`src/model/effects.ts` — the array the brief calls
`EFFECTS`) and their 165 declared parameters, plus `SynthParams`, `SamplerParams` and
`SampleZone`. The `wam` placeholder is out of scope: a plugin declares no parameters here,
by design.

Read against `src/model/effects.ts`, `src/audio/effectChain.ts`, `src/audio/exportMix.ts`,
`src/components/mixer/PluginFace.tsx`, `src/audio/synth.ts`,
`src/audio/samplerInstrument.ts`, `src/model/sampler.ts`, `src/model/synthFace.ts` and
`src/model/paramRegistry.ts`.

**Eighteen findings. Five are open — 1 high, 2 medium, 2 low. Thirteen were closed by
other work while the audit was running**, and are listed in full at the end so nobody
re-finds them and so a reader on an older branch knows what to expect. Line numbers are
from the final pass against a tree that moved repeatedly during the audit: check the quoted
text, not the number. Nothing here is asserted from a name — every row was read on both
sides. What was checked and found correct is listed last so it is not re-traced.

## Checked and found correct

Do not re-trace these.

**Every parameter reaches the audio or the readout, for all 27 kinds.** All 165 declared
parameters were traced from `ParamSpec.key` to the line that reads them. Two are read but
land on a node field the platform ignores (`eq8.lsQ`, `eq8.hsQ` — H-1). Three are read by
the face rather than by the graph, and correctly so, because on those devices the readout
_is_ the device: `gainMatch.target` (`matchTrimFor` in `src/model/effects.ts`, used by
`MatchFace`), `tuner.reference` (`TunerFace`) and `analyser.view` (the face's mode switch).
Nothing else is unread.

**Units and ranges against the DSP.** The Q-in-decibels trap is handled correctly
everywhere it arises in the effects: `buildFilter` converts with `qToDb` for the pass modes
and passes the plain factor to the bandpass, `buildEq8` does the same for HP and LP, and
`eqMagnitudeResponse` (`src/audio/dsp/curves.ts:223-240`) converts the same way for the
plots, so every face and its audio agree. Also checked and correct: `bits` stops at
`MAX_CRUSH_BITS`; `CRUSH_FACTORS` and the crusher's stage count agree at 64× and six
stages; `PHASER_STAGES` matches the `stages` range and step; the phaser's depth is cents
applied to `detune`; chorus and flanger depth is clamped to the base delay so the delay
line never goes negative; `DriveStage`'s maximum matches the Filter's `drive` range at
24 dB; every multiband band parameter is inside `DynamicsCompressorNode`'s own range; the
reverb's `size` range matches `renderImpulse`'s clamp and `predelay` fits its `DelayNode`.

**Faces.** `dynamicsLawOf`, `shaperCurveOf`, `deesserBand`, `multibandSplits`, `eq8Bands`,
`delayLayoutOf`, `reverbTailOf`, `widthFieldOf`, `modulationOf` and `tuneSettingsOf` are
each read by both the builder and the face, and each was checked on both sides. The
de-esser's band strip, the multiband's crossover, the four waveshaper curves, the reverb
envelope's 2.2 exponent and the Vocal Tune staircase all match the audio. Vocal Tune is
fully wired: all six parameters flow through `tuneSettingsOf` into
`src/components/audioeditor/AudioEditor.tsx:113-118`, and the `formantPreserve` flag
reaches `pitchShiftChannel`.

**Bypass.** Every insert was checked for a transparent bypass, including any latency it
adds, and all 27 now route around everything they add: `WetDry` forces dry to unity, the
control VCA crossfades to a constant-1 gain path with makeup and look-ahead returned to
unity, EQ8's gain bands ramp to 0 dB and its pass filters crossfade out, the Filter mutes
all three modes and opens a dry leg, the flanger's through-zero dry delay is forced off,
the limiter mutes its clipper leg, and the measurement inserts are a single unity gain with
a dead-end analyser branch. Only the compressor has a test for this
(`tests/effectCurves.test.ts:862`); widening it over every kind is the cheapest guard
against the next regression.

**Offline against realtime.** Both paths build every insert through the same
`InsertChain`/`buildEffectNode`, so there is no per-device divergence to find.
`exportMix.ts:429-445` mirrors `engine.buildMasterChain`, including the safety limiter's
threshold, knee, ratio and ballistics and the `master.limiter === false` disengage
(`engine.ts:667`); `:468-491` mirrors the channel strip; `:514-515` applies the same
sidechain routing; `:833-834` builds clip event-FX chains as the engine does at
`engine.ts:1386-1388`; instruments are constructed the same way at `:675-725`, and synth
and sampler automation is applied per voice at note time on both sides. The reverb's tail
is seeded from the effect id, so a bounce reproduces the monitored tail and two bounces
agree. The only divergences are the gain-reduction readouts — analyser-based, silent
offline, no effect on the audio — and the LFO phase (M-2).

**Instruments.** All nine `SynthParams` fields reach a `PolySynth` voice (`presetName` is
display only), and `synthFace.ts` reports the same clamps the voice applies, pinned by
`tests/synthFace.test.ts` including that the response is computed with Q as decibels. All
sixteen `SamplerParams` fields and all twenty-five `SampleZone` fields reach a voice or the
zone lookup: `matchZones` honours key and velocity ranges, mute, solo and round-robin
groups and applies the key crossfade; `zonePlaybackRate` uses `rootNote`, `keyTrack`,
`tuneCoarse` and `tuneFine`; the window, loop points and reverse mirroring are applied at
`samplerInstrument.ts:109-122`; `chokeGroup` cuts at `:99-103`; `oneShot`, `gain`, `pan`,
`velToGain` and the envelope all reach the voice. `zone.slices` is editor data by design,
consumed by the slice-to-pads and slice-to-MIDI actions in `projectStore.ts` rather than by
the voice. The "LFO → filter with the filter off" pair is already handled honestly:
`samplerLfoOf` returns null and the panel says why (`SamplerPanel.tsx:1222-1228`), though
the voice still constructs and starts an oscillator that connects to nothing — waste, not a
lie.

### Not covered

Third-party `wam` plugins, which declare no parameters here by design; note FX; and the
audio editor's offline retune beyond confirming that Vocal Tune's device settings drive it.
